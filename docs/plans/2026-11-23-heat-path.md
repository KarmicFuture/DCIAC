# Plan · 2026-11-23 · Heat path across views

## Problem

Heat is drawn on the floor and the rack, but nothing shows an operator how that heat moves from one screen to the next.

## Change

A **Heat path** button on every signed-in screen. It opens a panel and steps through Yard, Hall, Rack, One-line, Fleet, Control, Port map, and Sessions. Each step names what the heat is doing on that screen. Next, Back, and Stop are buttons. Escape stops it and returns focus to Heat path. After the first step it also advances on its own every 6 seconds, and stops on the last screen until Done or Stop.

## Out of scope

Changing the existing Simulate fault bar, raising a real thermal fault, or drawing new heat on the port map and sessions.
