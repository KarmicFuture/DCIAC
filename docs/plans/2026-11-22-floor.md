# Plan · 2026-11-22 · Floor: a keyboard "List" that opens the existing halls and racks panel

Branch: `ux/2026-11-21-cycle`  
Backlog item: **rank 2** in `docs/ux-backlog.md`, first slice only. The floor/site view depends on pointer picking, dragging, and non-focusable or `aria-hidden` clickable floor, pod, feed, and pipe elements, so keyboard and assistive-tech users can't reliably open halls, racks, servers, or alarm targets (WCAG 2.1.1, 4.1.2). Severity 4, effort L.

## Why this slice

- **Rank 2 is the only open item, and all of it doesn't fit one ~300-line cycle.** `src/views/floor.ts` has 2,158 lines, and the full item covers pods, aisles, feeds, pipes, focus order in a 3D-transformed world, camera follow, drag placement, and alarm focus.
- **The text lists already exist; they just can't be shown at the two entry levels.** `renderInspector()` in `src/views/floor.ts` already builds plain `<button>` lists:
  - Site level (lines 1815–1819): a "Ten halls" heading, a **Halls** list of 10 `floor-row` buttons (`data-act="floor-hall"`), and a **Yard** list of yard gear (`rowButton`, `data-act="floor-server"`).
  - Hall level (the `else` branch, lines 1855–1863): a **Racks** list of 20 `floor-row` buttons (`data-act="floor-rack"`), a **Power and cooling** list, and a **Watch list** of near, critical, and failover devices. Those are the alarm targets.
  - Pod, row, rack, server, part, and feed levels already show lists and crumbs in the same drawer.
  
  But `floorDetailOpen()` (lines 1897–1904) returns `false` at `hall` and at `site` without a selected server, and `goFloor("site"|"hall")` clears `state.inspectorOpen`. So the halls and racks lists are never shown at the two places where a user starts.
- **There is already a pattern to copy.** The HUD's "Add equipment" button toggles `state.libraryOpen`, and `mountScene()` (line 1906) appends that drawer. A "List" button that opens the existing inspector drawer at site and hall fits the same pattern. It needs no new picking model and no focusable SVG or 3D hit targets.
- **Without focus management, the drawer still wouldn't be usable.** `paint()` in `src/ui/render.ts` (line 254) re-renders the whole view through `unsafeHTML` on every `render()`, so focus falls to `<body>` after every list activation. `openRack()` also re-renders again after `loadElevation()`. Two small additions fix this: move focus into the drawer when it opens, and keep focus in the drawer across re-renders. Then a keyboard user can go from Outside to a hall, then a rack, then a device, without starting over from the top of the page each time.
- **The list items also need names that work.** Checked in Chrome: a `.floor-row` button such as `<i class="led" title="BMC"></i><span>GPU Tray 01</span><b>97%</b>` gets the accessible name `GPU Tray 01 97%`. The status dot's `title` is dropped, so the "BMC down" state, which is the alarm, is not announced. With `role="img" aria-label="BMC"` on the dot, the name becomes `BMC GPU Tray 01 97%`. In the rack list, the bare count (`16`) needs a word.
- **Estimate:** about 45 changed source lines and about 130 lines for one new spec, for roughly 175 lines in total.

## What remains of rank 2 after this slice

These stay open under rank 2 (see Out of scope):

- Pod plates (`.pod[data-act="floor-pod"]`) and aisle bands (`.aisle[data-act="floor-server"]`) in the 3D world are clickable `div`s that aren't focusable.
- Feeds and pipes (`feedHtml`, `pipeHtml`) are `aria-hidden` and pointer-only. Pipes aren't in the Yard list (`kind !== "Pipe"`).
- The 3D `.rackbox` targets are already `role="button" tabindex="0"` with Enter and Space support (`render.ts` line 720). But their Tab order follows DOM order, the camera doesn't follow focus, a focused box can be off-stage, and there are 20 or more tab stops before the drawer.
- Pan, orbit, and drag-to-place are pointer-only. Zoom has buttons, and placed items have nudge buttons, but a new library item can only be placed by dragging.
- `focusFloorAlarm()` moves the camera and pulses the rack but doesn't move keyboard focus or open the list on that rack.
- Focus is still lost on re-render everywhere outside the floor drawer.
- The hall list has no direct link to the hall overview (Rows, aisles, Nearing capacity). Today the only keyboard route there is the hall crumb in a rack's drawer.
- Nobody has done a live screen-reader pass (VoiceOver, NVDA) of the floor.

## Problem (today, from the code)

1. **There is no keyboard path to a list of halls or racks.** At `#/floor` and `#/floor/hall`, the scene shows the HUD and the 3D world only. `stage.spec.js` line 18 asserts `.inspector` has count 0. The only way to pick a hall or rack is a `.rackbox` inside a CSS-3D transformed `.world`, reached by tabbing through every box in DOM order.
2. **Focus is dropped after every action.** Activating any floor button calls `render()`. `paint()` replaces the view, and nothing puts focus back, so the next Tab starts at the skip link.
3. **Status isn't in the names.** `ledDot()` (line 1771) renders `<i class="led" title="…">`. That title doesn't contribute to the button's accessible name. In the hall **Racks** list, the device count is a bare number.
4. **`settle()` can't focus a container.** It runs `field.value.length` (render.ts line 75), which throws for an element with no `value`, such as an `<aside>`.

## User story

As a keyboard or screen-reader operator on the floor view, I want a **List** button that opens a panel listing the halls (outside) or the racks, power and cooling gear, and watch-listed devices (in a hall), each named with its status, and I want focus to stay in that panel as I drill in. That way I can reach a hall, rack, or alarming device without a mouse or the 3D scene.

## Change (for the builder)

Escape every interpolated value with `esc()`. Don't change pointer behavior.

1. **State.** Add `floorListOpen: false` to `src/state.ts` next to `libraryOpen` (line 128), and add `floorListOpen: boolean;` to `src/types.ts` next to `libraryOpen` (line 186). In `src/domain/demo.ts` line 110, where `state.libraryOpen = false;` is set, also set `state.floorListOpen = false;`.
2. **HUD button** (`hudHtml()`, `src/views/floor.ts` line 1020). Before `addBtn`, add the following, hidden while walking, the same way `addBtn` is:
   ```html
   <button type="button" class="act" id="floor-list-toggle" data-act="floor-list" aria-expanded="true|false">List</button>
   ```
   `aria-expanded` is `floorDetailOpen() ? "true" : "false"`.
3. **When the drawer shows** (`floorDetailOpen()`, line 1897):
   - Let the first guard pass when `state.floorListOpen` is true: `if (!state.inspectorOpen && !state.floorListOpen && !state.plan.selected && !state.plan.preview) return false;`.
   - At `site`: `return !!state.floor.serverId || state.floorListOpen;`.
   - At `hall`: `return state.floorListOpen;`. Keep `elevation` returning `false`.
   - Other levels are unchanged.
4. **`closeFloorDetail()`** (line 780): also set `state.floorListOpen = false`. Escape, a blank-scene click, and the toggle then all close list mode.
5. **The drawer element** (`renderInspector()` return, line 1865): change it to `<aside class="inspector drawer" id="floor-detail" tabindex="-1" aria-label="Floor details">`.
6. **Names.**
   - `ledDot()` (line 1771): add `role="img" aria-label="<tone.word>"` and keep `title`.
   - In the hall-level **Racks** list (line 1861) and the pod-level **Racks** list (line 1852), change the count to `n + '<span class="sr-only"> devices</span>'`. `.sr-only` already exists (styles.css line 989).
7. **The toggle action** (`src/ui/render.ts` `onClick` dispatch, next to `library-open`):
   ```ts
   else if (act === "floor-list") {
     if (floorDetailOpen()) { closeFloorDetail(); state.focusField = "floor-list-toggle"; }
     else { state.floorListOpen = true; state.focusField = "floor-detail"; }
     render();
   }
   ```
   Import `floorDetailOpen` from `../views/floor`.
8. **`settle()`** (render.ts line 75): guard the selection, so that it only calls `setSelectionRange` when `field.setSelectionRange` exists, and reads `(field.value || "").length`.
9. **Keep focus in the drawer across re-renders** (`paint()`, render.ts lines 239–262):
   - Before `litRender`, if `state.view === "floor"` and `document.activeElement` is inside `#floor-detail`, record a selector for it. The selector is `[data-act="…"]` plus whichever of `data-pod`, `data-rack`, `data-row`, `data-server`, `data-level`, `data-feed`, `data-part`, and `data-plan` it has. If the active element is the drawer itself, record `#floor-detail`.
   - After `settle()`, if a selector was recorded and `document.activeElement` is `<body>` or `null`, focus `#floor-detail <selector>` if it exists, otherwise `#floor-detail` if it exists. Use `{ preventScroll: true }`.
   - Don't do this when focus wasn't in the drawer before the render.
10. **Escape** (`onKeyDown`, render.ts line 713):
    - Add `state.floorListOpen` to the floor condition.
    - After `render()`, focus `#floor-list-toggle` if it exists, otherwise `#content`. `<main id="content" tabindex="-1">` already exists.
11. **CSS** (`styles.css`, next to `.scene .inspector.drawer`, line 1557): add `.scene .inspector.drawer:focus-visible { outline: 3px solid var(--focus); outline-offset: -3px; }`. The inset offset is needed because the drawer is flush with the scene's `overflow: hidden` edge.

Loading feedback: opening and closing the list is a synchronous local render. Opening a rack from the list reuses `openRack()`, which already renders the rack drawer at once and fills the elevation in later. No new action takes over 300 ms.

Empty states: the hall **Watch list** already shows "All rack A14 servers are nominal." when empty. The Halls and Racks lists are never empty.

## Acceptance criteria

All criteria are checked in one new spec, `e2e/floor-list.spec.js`. Setup, following `stage.spec.js`: use a 1440×900 viewport, `goto("/#/floor")`, sign in as `dsvs.operator`, and wait until `#app` doesn't contain "Loading the asset repository". Use keyboard input (`page.keyboard`) for every step marked "keyboard".

1. **It's closed by default and nothing else changes.** At `#/floor` and `#/floor/hall`, `.inspector` has count 0. `#floor-list-toggle` is a `<button>` with accessible name `List` and `aria-expanded="false"`. `stage.spec.js` and `hall.spec.js` pass unchanged.
2. **You can reach it with Tab.** Focus the HUD's `[data-act="floor-go"][data-level="hall"]` button. Within 6 presses of Tab (keyboard), `document.activeElement.id` is `floor-list-toggle`.
3. **Enter opens the site list and moves focus into it.** Press Enter (keyboard). Then:
   - `#floor-list-toggle` has `aria-expanded="true"`.
   - `#floor-detail` is visible, has role `complementary` and accessible name `Floor details`, and is `document.activeElement`.
   - The drawer contains the heading `Ten halls`, exactly 10 `[data-act="floor-hall"]` buttons, and the first one has accessible name matching `/^Hall 1\s+20 racks$/`.
   - Every `#floor-detail [data-act="floor-server"]` button (the Yard list) has an accessible name starting with one of `Nominal`, `Rise`, `Hot`, `RDP`, or `BMC`.
4. **Focus is visible on the drawer.** While `#floor-detail` is focused, `#floor-detail:focus-visible` matches, and its computed `outline-style` is not `none`.
5. **From a hall to its racks by keyboard.** Press Tab (keyboard) until `Hall 1` is focused (at most 4 presses, past the crumbs), then press Enter. Then:
   - `.hud-mission` matches `/Hall 1/`, and `#floor-detail` is still visible with heading `Hall 1`.
   - `#floor-detail [data-act="floor-rack"]` has count 20, and `[data-rack="A14"]` has an accessible name matching `/^(Nominal|Rise|Hot|RDP|BMC) .*A14.*\d+ devices$/`.
   - `document.activeElement` is `#floor-detail` or inside it.
6. **Alarm targets are listed.** In the hall drawer, the text `Watch list` is present, followed by at least one `[data-act="floor-server"]` button whose name starts with `BMC`, `RDP`, `Hot`, or `Rise`. If the seeded world has no such device at sign-in, set one up first the same way `alarm.spec.js` gets a P1 alarm. Don't add a test-only hook.
7. **Open a rack and keep focus.** Focus the `A14` button in the drawer and press Enter (keyboard). Then `#floor-detail .crumbs` contains `A14`. After the "Edit U positions" button (`[data-act="rack-positions"]`) is visible, which means the async elevation re-render has happened, `document.activeElement` is still `#floor-detail` or inside it, checked with `expect.poll` for about 1 s.
8. **Escape closes the list and returns focus.** Go back to `#/floor/hall`, open the list with the button (keyboard: focus it and press Enter), then press Escape. Then `.inspector` has count 0, `#floor-list-toggle` has `aria-expanded="false"`, and `#floor-list-toggle` is `document.activeElement`.
9. **The toggle closes the list.** With the list open at hall, focus `#floor-list-toggle` and press Enter (keyboard). Then `.inspector` has count 0, `aria-expanded="false"`, and focus is on `#floor-list-toggle`.
10. **Pointer behavior is unchanged.** Clicking `.rackbox[data-act="floor-rack"][data-rack="A14"] .ftop` at hall still opens the rack drawer with `A14` in the crumbs. These existing specs pass: `stage`, `hall`, `floor-drag`, `floor-rack3d`, `one-rack-view`, `device-view`, `alarm`, `lod`, `demo`, `full-demo`, `fix-demo`, `mp1000-demo`.
11. **The whole suite passes:** `npm test` in `prototypes/access-control` (`pretest` runs `vite build`).
